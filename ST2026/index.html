Welcome !

This workshop will bring together established researchers and early-career mathematicians working on different aspects of spectral theory, with the aim of strengthening collaborations and fostering a broader UK network in this area.

The list of invited speakers is:

Yan-Long Fang,UCL, UK
Noema Nicolussi, University Innsbruck, Austria

Leonid Pastur, King's College, UK
Alexander Pushnitski, King's College, UK
Jakob Reiffenstein, Stockholm University, Sweden
Mira Shamis, QMUL, UK & HIT, Israel
Eugene Shargorodsky, King's College, UK
