Selected publications

Chebyshev polynomials on a Jordan arc, with B. Buchecker and O. Rubin and A. Wennman, (submitted). [arXiv]

We prove Szegő–Widom asymptotics for the Chebyshev polynomials of an analytic Jordan arc, resolving a problem that has remained open since Widom's 1969 work.

  • Chebyshev polynomials on a Jordan arc, with B. Buchecker and O. Rubin and A. Wennman, (submitted). [arXiv]

    We prove Szegő–Widom asymptotics for the Chebyshev polynomials of an analytic Jordan arc, resolving a problem that has remained open since Widom's 1969 work.

  • Necessary and sufficient conditions for universality limits, with M. Lukic and H. Woracek, to appear in Invent. Math.(to appear). [arXiv]

    We derive the first necessary and sufficient conditions for universality limits for fixed measures. Our results cover prominent universality classes such as bulk universality, hard-edge universality, and universality for measures with Fisher–Hartwig singularities.

  • An approach to universality using Weyl m-functions, with M. Lukic and B. Simanek, Ann. of Math. (2). [article]

    We prove the first pointwise, purely local sufficient condition for bulk universality limits. To do so, we introduce a new framework for studying scaling limits of orthogonal polynomials via canonical systems.

  • Stahl-Totik regularity for continuum Schrödinger operators, with M. Lukic, Anal. PDE. [article]

    We develop a theory of Stahl–Totik regularity for unbounded operators. Using the Martin compactification, we identify a constant associated with the spectrum of the operator that plays the role of a renormalized Robin constant for unbounded spectra.

  • KdV hierarchy via Abelian coverings and operator identities, with T. VandenBoom and P. Yuditskii, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. Ser. B. [article]

    We develop a method that extends the classical study of algebro-geometric solutions of the KdV equation to non-compact Riemann surfaces. We use this theory to prove that solutions of the KdV equation with reflectionless initial data satisfying a finite gap length condition are almost periodic in time. This confirms, for a large class of initial data, a conjecture of Deift that almost periodic initial data yield solutions that are almost periodic in time. The conjecture was later disproved in full generality by Chapouto, Killip, and Vişan in Duke Math. J.
